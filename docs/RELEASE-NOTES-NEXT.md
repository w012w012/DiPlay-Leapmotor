## Leapmotor C11 fork

- fix24 conservatively removes the BYD SOME/IP HUD sender/icons, BYD lighting and music-analysis hooks, and HUD-only debug bridges from this C11 fork. Old BYD vehicle, speedometer, tyre, navigation-mode, instrument-output and popup preferences cannot activate those integrations on C11; settings and setup no longer offer them. Shared iPhone route/call state, C11 map/music/turn cards, optional default-off C11 logs, audio/video policy, ADB, and recovery journals remain. Generic Siri/controller, navigation-volume, Bluetooth suspension and independently detected FCE controls stay available. No settings reset is required. See [cleanup boundaries and verification](C11-FIX24-CLEANUP.md).
- fix23 expands both C11 map position sliders to 5–95% for every size, while displaying directional pixel offsets from the reset layout centre and labelling the physical slider ends. Near an edge, the iPhone route-fit area may shrink; the full 720x720 background, window and fades remain fixed. Existing fix22 positions keep the same meaning, and changes still require reconnecting. See [manual map layout](C11-MANUAL-MAP-LAYOUT.md).
- As explicitly requested, C11 vehicle-log reading is now off by default, with an opt-in control in Advanced. Off prevents all fast, slow and event-census logcat scans and C11 battery advertisement; it also removes log-based reverse music pause/resume and speed readings. C11 also ignores stale BYD battery-preload and wheel-speed preferences, retaining normal GPS. Passive broadcasts, steering-wheel broadcasts, multimedia ownership heartbeats and maps remain. The control applies on the next connection. C11 diagnostics add one process CPU/heap/memory/UI-delay summary every 30 seconds; video parking, frame rate and recovery policy are unchanged for a controlled comparison. Car-wide performance causality is still unverified; see [performance investigation](C11-FIX22-PERFORMANCE-20261016.md).
- fix22 replaces the C11 map size presets with continuous map size, horizontal centre and vertical centre sliders plus reset. Changes apply after reconnecting; the 720x720 window, full video and transparent fades remain fixed. Positions were bounded without shrinking the selected fit box; fix23 expands this range with edge refitting. Old presets map to 100/150/175/50, settings travel through layout backups, and the fix19 pixel-offset probe and fix21 moving-mask approach are removed. The C11 fixed-rate audio correction remains. iPhone/navigation-app acceptance is pending; see [manual map layout](C11-MANUAL-MAP-LAYOUT.md).
- fix21 keeps the left/top alpha ramps and their rounded joint aligned with the translated map pixels inside the stationary cluster window. It also disables pitch-changing music clock trim on the C11 firmware family: fix19 diagnostics showed the 48 kHz track being driven between 47,280 and 48,709 Hz. Music keeps its negotiated rate; buffering, rebuffer recovery, stale-packet protection and microphone fixes remain. Other head units retain the existing clock controller. C11 listening/visual acceptance is pending; see [fix19 diagnosis](C11-FIX19-DIAGNOSIS-20261016.md).
- fix20 removes the retired colour-band edge overlay and its setting, palette/night-mode hooks and ADB-route overlay. Transparent fading and the off choice remain. Existing saved colour-mode value 1 is interpreted as transparency; saved off value 2 stays off. Fade widths, corner geometry and the fix19 map-offset probe remain unchanged. The fix19 test APK is preserved separately for the planned car test.
- fix19 adds a reversible cluster picture offset probe, enabled by default: the decoded map video moves 15% right and 10% down (108×72 px on the 720×720 square), without changing its scale, the CarPlay safe area, the fixed edge fade or local overlay cards. In Advanced → Dashboard map, turn off **Cluster picture offset (experimental)** to compare at the original position immediately. The probe requires the active transparent fade renderer; restart DiPlay after changing fade modes. Right/bottom content may be cropped. This tests local pixel placement, not Amap camera zoom, and awaits C11 acceptance.
- Music clock correction now includes compressed audio waiting for decode. Sustained stale music is trimmed after a grace period; short Wi-Fi catch-up bursts and non-music streams are preserved. Audio rebuffer reports are capped to keep diagnostic exports bounded.
- Voice-message microphone capture can start when the iPhone sends no downlink audio. A recorder returning empty reads is restarted up to three times, with a short pause between reads. Calls still wait for downlink audio as their echo reference.
- Crash reports retain up to six additional DiPlay stack frames when Android frames fill the usual report window.

These changes have automated coverage. Acceptance on a Leapmotor C11 is pending.

## Display

- With **Efficient video** (HEVC) on, a head unit without a hardware HEVC decoder now gets H.264. Android had picked its own software decoder instead: a Spreadtrum SC7731E head unit (Android 8.1) decoded every session in software with `OMX.google.hevc.decoder` while DiPlay logged `decoder=hardware`, and music skipped ([#727](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/DiPlay/issues/727)). The report says `HEVC is on, but no hardware decoder plays it`. **HEVC software decoder** still keeps HEVC on Android 10 and later.
